BRING YOUR OWN KEY

Try a free API tier, then choose the model quality you need.

WriteMelo does not sell credit or require a subscription. Basic models are enough to test the workflow; stronger models usually work better for important email and long-form writing.

1Create a provider account→2Use a free tier without auto-billing→3Connect it in WriteMelo
Why is a key needed?

Think of WriteMelo as the appliance and the AI provider as metered electricity. The key authorizes only your provider account.

Must I pay now?

No. Look for an explicit free API plan or credit. If you do not want to spend, do not enable automatic billing.

Can it keep charging?

WriteMelo cannot charge you. Review bills and set limits in the provider console; usage stops when no credit remains.

MODEL QUALITY

Use speed for simple work and capability for complex work

Free or basic

Good for short completion, simple corrections and testing the app. Long-context consistency and subtle tone may vary.

Balanced

Good for everyday email and routine polishing. Compare latency, context length and your actual output.

Higher quality

Usually better at preserving intent, formal tone, complex rewriting and explanations in important or long writing.

Price is not proof

Test the same real passage across completion, review and polish before deciding whether a paid model is worthwhile.

PRIVACY AND COST

Your key stays local

WriteMelo does not receive the key

It is submitted only to 127.0.0.1 and protected with Windows DPAPI.

The provider receives necessary text

Your computer sends writing context directly to the provider to generate results.

The provider charges per usage

Token pricing and refunds are controlled by the provider, not WriteMelo.

The key is not cloud-synced

Configure it again on a new PC. Clearing local app data removes the saved key.
